# Establish readiness

Read the configured readiness references and requirements. Confirm preceding
required stages passed against current evidence and that required verification
and cleanup are complete. No unresolved blocking question can be hidden by a
ready label. An issue can bypass verification only through its configured
not-required transition with an evidence-backed rationale.

For an issue, establish that scope, intended behavior, relevant environment and
acceptance criteria are clear enough to begin work. For a PR, establish that
review and verification support maintainer acceptance. Record a concise brief
with evidence, limits and any nonblocking questions.

Ready is a local result. Propose its configured label and, when useful, an updated
public understanding summary. Publishing either requires separate authorization.
Do not implement a fix, close an item, submit an approval review or merge solely
because this stage passed.
