# Classify and consult history

Read the configured references and requirements. Establish intent, category,
affected area, repository scope, existing implementations and related issues/PRs.
Review relevant accepted and rejected contributions. Cite concrete similarities
and differences; a past rejection is not a blanket policy.

For issues, decide whether reproduction or another verification is needed. Bug
claims generally need evidence of actual versus expected behavior; feature ideas
may need product clarification instead. Follow configured rules. Record
`verificationNeed` as `required`, `notRequired` with rationale, or `undetermined`
when essential information is missing. Undetermined is not permission to skip.
Check duplicates and supported-version evidence without closing anything.

For PRs, establish whether this is a wanted contribution under accepted scope,
what it changes, why it is needed and whether required issue links exist. Treat
author intent and contributor-provided tests as evidence, not a passing review.
Unsettled product decisions need maintainer clarification.

No contributor execution. Record facts, hypotheses, history and the next
verification/review questions in the item understanding before progressing.
