# Our understanding

## Intent and current behavior

Explain the problem or contribution in our own terms. Separate reported behavior
from what we actually observed, and identify the assessed revision.

## Applicable rules and related history

Name configured requirement IDs and references. Link similar issues/PRs and
maintainer decisions; distinguish history from accepted policy.

## Evidence and hypotheses

Record decisive evidence with sources. Clearly mark hypotheses and unknowns.

## Verification

State whether verification is required and why. List actual source/component
versions, reproduction steps, checks run/results, limitations and cleanup.
Keep planned checks explicitly separate from executed checks.

## Current assessment and next action

Explain stage/disposition, unresolved questions and what resolves them. Link
pending proposals and the last approved public summary when available.

## Changes in understanding

Retain consequential earlier interpretations and explain why new evidence changed
them. Do not carry stale findings forward as current conclusions.
